About this data

Prices are from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP), captured midday Nov 6, 2026. Yield is the trailing annual dividend FMP reports divided by price. The leveraged ETFs' payouts are small and irregular. About this data

Prices are closes on Nov 5, 2026. BXSL's yield uses its $0.77 regular quarterly dividend ($3.08 a year) over price; its Q3 2026 NAV was $25.25. TLT's distribution yield uses the twelve monthly payouts from Nov 2025 to Oct 2026 ($3.89). The 20-yr Treasury yield (5.27% on Nov 5) stands in for TLT's yield to maturity. "Return" here means income yield at today's prices. It leaves out price changes, which for TLT can be large because of its long duration, and BXSL's dividend is not guaranteed. Weights drift as prices move, so the mix needs periodic rebalancing to hold the target. This is a model, not investment advice.
